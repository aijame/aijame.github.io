Token Valuation & Financial Ratio

Every AI company began working on large language models (LLM) foundations in a token economy. The token input and output has different cost for them and users. On one hand, the AI models with more inaccuracies will push users to explain with more tokens in prompt and more frequecies. This will increase the cost and reduce cost efficiency of the AI company. On the other hand, the more smarter model with less errors, reduce the cost and increase the efficiency. This means that the value of that AI company per less token and less energy use will be higher to reduce burning rate. In addition, the smarter the models are, the more interface intelligence intensity will contribute to that company. Therefore, we define a new token term as company's value per token (VPT).

The VPT is calculated based on token cost economy (TCE) parameters and corporate interface intelligence (CII) demonstrated statistically in Figure 4 for one venture simulation. The interface intelligence is also quantize based on cb, βb, and cm βm. For CII, the cb is modeled with numbers of biological cognitive abilities such as number of employees of that AI company. βb is modeled with biological modalities of communication fidelity such as abilities like human visions, olfactory sensing, human auditory sensors, linguistics, brain interface machine developments. Therefore for companies like Neurolink, the βb = 3 is considered in simulation setup and for other human corporations without brain interface machines, the βb = 2 is considered.

For corporate machines, βm can be more modalities such as text, images, sounds, brain interface machine, transponders, infrared camera, piezoelectric, etc. Thus, for computer simulation, we considered βm = 7 as a based line parameter setup. And finally for cm we can assume number of GPU units available multiplied by LLM model score the AI company possess. The TCE for all AI companies is based on AI model efficiency, we assumed same as for AI companies for this simulation setup around $0.003.
